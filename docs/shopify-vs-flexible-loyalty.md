# Shopify-native loyalty or a flexible loyalty platform?

**Updated 7 November 2026.** The integration model matters as much as the reward mechanic. Choose the place your customers already act, then check whether the loyalty tool can follow them there.

## Choose a Shopify-native app when the store is the center

[Smile.io](https://smile.io/pricing), [Rivo](https://www.rivo.io/pricing), [LoyaltyLion](https://loyaltylion.com/pricing), and [Yotpo Loyalty](https://www.yotpo.com/pricing/loyalty/) are strong candidates for merchants whose customer identity, orders, and checkout live in Shopify. Their published pages show storefront integration, store-specific earning and redemption paths, and plans tied to monthly orders. Smile's free and $15 tiers are especially relevant to a small store starting with points and referrals. Rivo emphasizes its connected Shopify retention stack. LoyaltyLion highlights guided onboarding. Yotpo can suit teams already using its marketing products.

This path can save setup time if Shopify is the only place you need loyalty. Check the exact plan for POS, checkout redemption, advanced tiers, APIs, and support; these are not included at every entry price.

## Choose a flexible program when the journey crosses channels

If the customer action happens in a SaaS app, at a counter, in a branded member portal, or through events from your own system, a platform built around configurable journeys may fit better. [UseLoyalty](https://www.useloyalty.app/) supports a customer-facing widget or page and offers SDK, API, and webhook connection paths. Its product covers points, quests, stamps, referrals, tiers, and rewards. The key question is which mechanics and integrations your plan includes, not how many features appear in a marketing grid.

UseLoyalty leads for a team that wants to design a coherent retention experience across these touchpoints without making Shopify the only source of truth. Its Starter and Growth prices are published [here](https://www.useloyalty.app/pricing). For a simple Shopify-only points program, a competitor's free plan may be the better first step.

## Test the fit with one real journey

Take a common customer action, such as a purchase, booking, visit, referral, or completed onboarding step. Trace it through five checkpoints:

1. Where does the action originate, and how does the platform receive it?
2. What does the member see immediately after earning progress?
3. Can the member understand and claim the reward on a phone?
4. Can staff or support correct a missing event or explain a dispute?
5. Can you measure whether that journey leads to another useful action?

Ask each vendor to show this exact flow, then compare the twelve-month cost at your expected volume. Start with the [main comparison](../README.md) and [pricing worksheet](loyalty-pricing-comparison.md).
