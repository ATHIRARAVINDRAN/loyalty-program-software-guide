Smile.io, LoyaltyLion, and Yotpo also publish free plans. For a small Shopify store that only needs basic rewards, those options can have a lower cash cost than UseLoyalty. That is a good reason to compare the **actual member experience and required features**, rather than just the plan names.

## What “10× less” can and cannot mean

Smile.io lists Plus at **$999/month with annual billing**. UseLoyalty Growth is **$99/month with monthly billing**. The ratio is approximately 10.1 to 1. This is a valid comparison of two published prices; it is **not a like-for-like comparison**. Smile Plus is aimed at much larger Shopify brands and includes a different order allowance, service package, and API access. UseLoyalty's Enterprise plan is custom-priced. We recommend against using “10× cheaper than competitors” as a general headline.

The more useful message for a buyer is: **Launch a branded loyalty experience with the mechanics you need, then pay for the plan that fits your team.** UseLoyalty publishes its two standard prices, and you can test the customer journey before choosing a larger plan.

## Estimate your own 12-month cost

1. Record your current monthly orders, locations, members, and expected growth.
2. List must-have features: stamps, points, referrals, tiers, quests, wallet passes, API, POS, or messaging.
3. Find the lowest plan that includes those features. Check whether pricing grows with orders or locations.
4. Add implementation work, SMS or push charges, migration, and other paid services.
5. Compare the total with the incremental gross profit you reasonably expect from repeat visits or purchases.
